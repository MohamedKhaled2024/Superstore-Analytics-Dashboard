# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence** project based on the Superstore dataset.

The project combines **Python** for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and business insights with **Power BI** to transform those insights into an interactive and professional dashboard.

The main objective is to understand **sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends**, and translate the findings into actionable business recommendations.

---
## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions:

* How are **Sales and Profit** performing over time?
* Which **categories and sub-categories** generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Which **products** are driving business performance?
* Which **regions** contribute the most to Sales and Profit?
* How do different **customer segments** perform?
* Where are the main **profitability gaps and improvement opportunities**?
* How can the analysis support better **data-driven business decisions**?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis uncovered several important business insights across sales, profitability, customers, products, and discounts.

### 💰 Overall Performance

* **Total Sales:** $2.30M
* **Total Profit:** $286K
* **Profit Margin:** 12.47%
* **Total Orders:** 5,009
* **Total Customers:** 793
* **Units Sold:** 37,873

These KPIs provide a high-level view of the overall business performance and serve as the foundation for deeper analysis.

### 📦 Category Performance

The **Technology** category was the strongest contributor to both Sales and Profit, highlighting its importance to overall business performance.

However, high revenue did not always translate into high profitability across all products and sub-categories.

### 📉 Discount & Profitability

The analysis identified a clear relationship between **discount levels and profitability**.

Some highly discounted products generated significant sales but resulted in very low or negative profit, indicating that aggressive discounting can negatively impact business performance.

### 👤 Customer Profitability

Customer-level analysis revealed that revenue alone is not enough to evaluate customer value.

For example:

**Sean Miller**

* Sales: **$25,043**
* Profit: **-$1,981**
* Discount: **24.7%**

This demonstrates how a high-value customer in terms of revenue can still negatively impact profitability.

### 🛒 Product-Level Performance

Several products generated strong sales but negative profit.

A notable example is:

**Cisco TelePresence EX90**

* Sales: **$22,638**
* Profit: **-$1,811**
* Discount: **50%**

This highlights the importance of monitoring **product profitability alongside sales performance**.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customer segmentation identified **405 customers** within the **Low Sales – Low Profit** segment.

This creates an opportunity to evaluate whether these customers can be developed through targeted offers, improved retention strategies, or more efficient acquisition efforts.

### ⚠️ Loss-Making Products

The analysis also identified products with significant negative profitability, including:

**Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print**

These products require further investigation into pricing, discounting, product cost, and overall commercial strategy.

---

### 🎯 Key Takeaway

The analysis demonstrates that **Sales alone do not provide a complete picture of business performance**.

Combining Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Discounts, Customers, and Products provides a much clearer understanding of where the business is creating value — and where performance needs improvement.
