# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence** project based on the Superstore dataset.

The project combines **Python** for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and business insights with **Power BI** to transform those insights into an interactive and professional dashboard.

The main objective is to understand **sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends**, and translate the findings into actionable business recommendations.

---
## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions:

* How are **Sales and Profit** performing over time?
* Which **categories and sub-categories** generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Which **products** are driving business performance?
* Which **regions** contribute the most to Sales and Profit?
* How do different **customer segments** perform?
* Where are the main **profitability gaps and improvement opportunities**?
* How can the analysis support better **data-driven business decisions**?
